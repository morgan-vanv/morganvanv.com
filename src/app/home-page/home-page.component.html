Hey there! Welcome to my personal website. Feel free to explore and connect with me.

What's going on here?

This website originally started as a simple project to showcase my projects and experience in a professional context - a classic portfolio website. After some time, this began to feel uninspired, and limiting to me. I lost motivation and put the project down for a while.

However, I have recently become reinspired by the IndieWeb, Web Revival movements, neocities, and the like. So I decided to re-approach this project with those inspirations in mind.

This is still a work in progress, so be sure to check back in sometime and see what has changed! And if you are here for professional reasons, don't worry - all of that information is still here.

About Me

So, I'm really just some guy. I grew up in the Greater Cincinnati area (where I recently moved back to), I studied Physics & Applied Mathematics at Ohio University, and I currently work as a Software Engineer for The Home Depot.

Outside of that, I mostly spend my time listening to music and podcasts, tinkering with tech stuff, and playing Old School Runescape. I also dabble in photography (astrophotography when I can), collecting vinyl, and even cycling on occasion.
